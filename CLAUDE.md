# Home: Equestria

Godot 4.5 GDScript pony game with LLM-driven ponies. Before working here read
[AGENT_HANDOFF.md](AGENT_HANDOFF.md): environment limits (no private assets in the cloud checkout),
how to compile-check and test headless, how to ship a `zz_patch.pck` to the user, the
chemistry and AI-action architecture, and the age/safety rules that must not be weakened.
